# ZTT Partnership Sales Builder's Guide

> *A builder's guide to partnership-driven sales — for trainers becoming visionary advocates.*

---

## What This Is

This is an internal training tool for **Zero to Three (ZTT)** trainers who are learning to lead the professional development partnership sales process — from the first inquiry to a long-term learning relationship.

It is not a traditional sales script. It is a **vision-aligned, builder-centered framework** that teaches trainers how to listen for a client's north star, honor the assets already present in their team, and position ZTT as a co-builder — not a vendor.

---

## What's Inside

The guide walks through **14 stages across 7 phases:**

| Phase | Stages |
|---|---|
| 1 · Lead Intake | Inquiry → Qualify & Schedule |
| 2 · Discovery | Pre-call Research → Discovery Call → Follow-up |
| 3 · Proposal | AI-assisted Draft → Gamma.app Build → Revision |
| 4 · Contract | Development → Signing |
| 5 · Onboarding | Planning Kickoff → Content Creation |
| 6 · Delivery | Touchpoints → Program Delivery |
| 7 · Growth | Debrief → Continued Learning & Upsell |

Each stage is **clickable and expandable** — tap any card to reveal:
- Step-by-step checklists
- Builder-framed discovery questions
- The **magic wand question** framework
- PLAUD recording workflow
- A ready-to-copy **AI prompt** for generating proposals
- The **Gamma.app** visual proposal workflow
- Decision forks and follow-up cadences

---

## How to Use This Guide

**For trainers in training:**
Work through each phase in order. Click every stage card and read the detail before your first sales conversation. Pay special attention to Stage 03 (Discovery Call) — this is where partnerships are built or lost.

**Before a discovery call:**
Open Stage 03. Review the pre-call research checklist, the magic wand questions, and the PLAUD workflow. Have it open on your phone or a second screen during the call.

**After a discovery call:**
Open Stage 05. Use the copy button to grab the AI prompt, fill in your client's details, paste your cleaned PLAUD notes, and generate your proposal draft. Then take it into Gamma.app to build the visual deck.

**As a reference:**
Bookmark the live URL. Return to any stage whenever you need a reminder of what comes next or how to handle a specific moment in the process.
